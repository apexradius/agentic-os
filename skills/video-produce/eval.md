# Eval: video-produce (rubric)

End-to-end video production is graded on the assembled result, not a single clip. Score against
the rubric.

## Task
> "Produce a 30s product-demo video for a meal-kit service — generation, editing, and assembly
> into a finished cut."

## Rubric
| # | Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Brief adherence | 3 | Finished cut matches subject, length (≈30s), and purpose (demo) |
| 2 | Assembly quality | 2 | Clips assembled into a coherent edit (transitions, trims), not raw output |
| 3 | Structure/pacing | 2 | A deliberate narrative arc; holds attention, no dead air |
| 4 | Audio sync | 2 | Voiceover/music/captions aligned to the visuals |
| 5 | Platform spec | 1 | Aspect ratio, duration, and format fit the destination |

Total: 10 points.

## Pass threshold
≥ 8 of 10. A ★ criterion scoring 0 (finished cut off-brief) fails regardless of total.
