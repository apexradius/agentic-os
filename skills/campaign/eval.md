# Eval: campaign (rubric)

A campaign is graded on cross-asset coherence and completeness, not one asset. Score against the
rubric.

## Task
> "Plan a launch campaign for a new note-taking app — research, creative, ad copy, email
> sequence, social content."

## Rubric
| # | Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Grounded brief | 2 | A creative brief tied to research (audience, angle), not invented |
| 2 ★ | Message consistency | 3 | One value prop carried consistently across every asset |
| 3 | Channel fit | 2 | Each asset tailored to its channel, not one copy reused everywhere |
| 4 | Completeness | 2 | The requested assets (ad copy, email sequence, social) are all produced |
| 5 | Measurement | 1 | A CTA and a success metric/attribution defined |

Total: 10 points.

## Pass threshold
≥ 8 of 10. A ★ criterion scoring 0 (no grounded brief, or inconsistent messaging across assets)
fails regardless of total.
