# Market Scan

## Steps
1. **WebSearch** for market size, growth rate, key players
2. **Analyze competitors** — pricing models, positioning, strengths/weaknesses
3. **Identify trends** — emerging technologies, regulatory changes, consumer shifts
4. **Map pain points** — what customers complain about (review sites, forums)
5. **Revenue resilience check**: classify market revenue as Fixed (licenses/retainers), Predictable (subscriptions), or Variable (project-based) — fixed/predictable = higher valuation multiples
6. **Output**: executive summary, competitive landscape, opportunity analysis, entry barriers, recommended positioning

## Opportunity Navigator Matrix
Classify strategic opportunities by:
- **Gold Mine**: High potential + Low challenge → prioritize immediately
- **Moonshot**: High potential + High challenge → resource carefully, long timeline
- **Quick Win**: Low potential + Low challenge → execute fast, low investment
- **Questionable**: Low potential + High challenge → avoid or kill

## Evidence-Based Validation (for new market entry)
Don't rely on spreadsheets and opinion — grade evidence by strength:
- Level 0: Internal spreadsheets (no evidence)
- Level 1: Customer interviews / surveys (light "say")
- Level 2: Prototypes, landing pages with copy (artifact "say")
- Level 3: Ad clicks, email sign-ups (light "do")
- Level 4: Down payments, letters of intent (strong "do")
- Level 5: Pre-sales, pop-up shops, live orders (irrefutable)

Only invest significantly at Level 4+.

## Anti-Patterns
- **Analysis Paralysis**: Weeks on internal spreadsheets for unproven ideas → get out and test with real people first
- **Lighthouse Bets**: Single massive bet on one opportunity → diversify across a funnel; expect 70% to fail
- **Data as Power Fallacy**: Collecting vast data without a business model to interpret and apply it

## Tools
- Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas (strategic mapping)
- Similar Web (competitor traffic analysis)
- Clay (high-volume data research and vendor waterfall)
- Perplexity (verified market search)
