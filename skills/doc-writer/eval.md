# Eval: doc-writer (rubric)

Long-form documents are graded on structure, accuracy, and readability, not one right text.
Score against the rubric.

## Task
> "Write a technical guide: 'Migrating a REST API to GraphQL — a practical guide.'"

## Rubric
| # | Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Structure | 2 | Logical heading hierarchy with a clear progression |
| 2 ★ | Accuracy | 3 | Claims are correct and, where needed, sourced — not confidently wrong |
| 3 | Audience fit | 2 | Depth matches the stated reader (practitioner), neither shallow nor bloated |
| 4 | Examples | 1 | Concrete examples / code where they aid understanding |
| 5 | Readability | 1 | Scannable; no padding |
| 6 | No AI tells | 1 | Free of formulaic AI phrasing ("in today's…", "delve", rote rule-of-three) |

Total: 10 points.

## Pass threshold
≥ 8 of 10. A ★ criterion scoring 0 (no real structure, or a factual error) fails regardless of
total.
