Corpus skews to the 2025–2026 automation era (smart bidding, AI Max, Advantage+/Andromeda). **Numbers are SOURCE CLAIMS, not verified fact** — CPCs, CTRs, ROAS lifts, conversion thresholds drift by sector/date; treat as directional and re-verify against the account's own baseline. ## §structure — account & campaign architecture

- Core shift: hyper-granular legacy structures (SKAGs, one-keyword ad groups) are now a **hidden cost** — they starve smart-bidding of data density; rational under manual bidding, obsolete under semantic matching + creative automation [GA1]. Smart-bidding/pCTR models key on **semantic features, not campaign/ad-group IDs**, so moving creatives between groups does NOT trigger relearning — "learning-phase fear" is largely a myth on Google [GA1].
- Structure to your **business (P&Ls, product lines, regions, distinct bidding goals)**, not your keyword list; there is no "perfect" structure [GA1]. Default to **consolidation** to pool conversions; segment only when a fundamental business difference demands it [GA1].
- Ad groups = **tightly-themed distinct concepts**, judged qualitatively: if two ad groups share landing page + campaign + goal + overlapping intent, merge them [GA1]. Keywords are a **thematic layer / means to an end**, not the target — run lean ad groups of a handful of 1–2-word broad keywords, keep exact only for strict intent control [GA1][GA2].
- Migrate legacy accounts safely: map the lean end-state, sandbox on low-priority campaigns, roll out in batches — never restructure a whole account at once, avoid peak season [GA1].
- Meta mirrors this: **less is more** — few campaigns/ad sets, combine cold+warm into one consolidated hybrid ad set so conversions concentrate; scattered ad sets cause audience overlap and starve the learning phase [MA17][MA18]. Meta's targeting is now suggestion-based, so many ad sets end up chasing the same people anyway [MA18].

## §targeting

- **Google** control is no longer manual bids — it is: pass high-quality conversion/first-party data, set accurate smart-bidding targets, and use **brand controls, geo controls, negative keywords, term exclusions** [GA1]. Match types are a spectrum: exact (strict intent) → phrase → broad/AI Max (theme signal) [GA1]. Broad + keywordless matching is a **prerequisite to appear in AI Overviews / AI Mode** — exact-match keywords are ineligible there [GA1].
- **Meta**: creative is the primary targeting tool — the algorithm reads who stops/engages and finds lookalike prospects [MA4]. Detailed interests are treated as **suggestions, not boundaries** (and are ~30% inaccurate / increasingly deleted) [MA4]. **Advantage+ Audience** = inputs are "suggestions"; to hard-constrain you must click "further limit reach" and deselect the suggestion option [MA4].
- **Go broad (AI-era default)** for conversion-focused e-comm/leads to feed volume and hit the learning threshold [MA17]. **Constrain** in exactly 3 cases: geo-restricted local/service businesses (out-of-market waste), regulated verticals (gambling/finance/housing/health), and hyper-specific contextual campaigns (abandoned-cart, webinar-reminder to registrants) [MA4].
- **Lookalikes** depend on source funnel depth (buyers ≫ page-viewers); source lists want ~1,000+ matched profiles post-iOS14.5; larger (5%+) lookalikes now often beat 1–2% [MA-audience][MA7]. **Meta-source** warm audiences (video views, lead-form opens, page/IG engagement) are unaffected by iOS14.5 and are cheap/accurate, unlike Pixel/customer-list "your sources" [MA7].

## §bidding-budget

- Google smart-bidding data floor: **~15 conversions / 30 days** per campaign (baseline); **~25–30** for tCPA/lead-gen or a tCPA campaign "dies out" [GA1][GA2]. Pool sub-threshold campaigns (same goal/action) into a **portfolio bid strategy + shared budget**, then **do not touch it for 30 days** [GA1][GA13].
- Strategy fit: **Max Conversions** when all leads are ~equal value; **Max Conversion Value / tROAS** when values vary and you can pass accurate/predictive values back; **tCPA** for a specific cost-per-action; **Manual/Max Clicks** for demand-generation on best-converting terms or cold new accounts [GA3][GA12]. Common two-lane play: automated (demand-capture) + Max Clicks (demand-generation) in parallel [GA12].
- Track as far down the funnel as still clears the volume threshold; if the true sale is rare/delayed, optimize to a fast, frequent **mid-funnel proxy** (qualified call, booked consult) [GA2][GA5].
- **Meta learning phase**: ~**50 conversion events / week / ad set** to exit and avoid "Learning Limited"; fix via consolidation or optimizing a higher-funnel event (Add-to-Cart vs Purchase) for low-budget/high-ticket [MA15]. **CBO/Advantage Campaign Budget** = default (algo distributes across ad sets); **ABO** = manual per-ad-set control [MA16].
- Budgets: prefer **daily** (flexible, scalable, no reset) over **lifetime** (lumpy; required only for dayparting/ad-scheduling and ad-sequencing) [MA13]. Scale **vertically** (raise budget on winners) in small steps (~3%/day via automated rules) to avoid resetting learning; scale **horizontally** (new avatars, angles, larger lookalikes, broad interests) once vertical caps hit [MA16].

## §creative

- Post-automation, **offer + creative are the only real competitive levers** left on Meta [MA1]. Creative *is* the targeting.
- **Hook = first 1–3 seconds**, the highest-leverage variable — **90–95% never watch past it** [MA3]. Track **hook rate** (3-sec views ÷ impressions) [MA-creative]. Record **10 hooks per body** → ~20 ads for the price of 3, which also beats ad fatigue [MA3].
- **UGC / imperfect / creator "partnership ads"** outperform polished production — authenticity + borrowed trust lift hook rate and conversion [MA1]. Use **pattern interrupts** and **match cuts** to hold past 3s [MA6].
- Formats: source claims **Reels (vertical 9×16) ≈ +13% ROAS / −16% CPA** vs other placements; keep ≤60s for full placement eligibility [MA5]. But **~60–70% of conversions still happen on static**, and **carousels are resurgent** — run a mix [MA2].
- **Andromeda rewards creative diversity and punishes near-duplicate variations with higher CPMs**; aim for **~20 distinct creatives live per ad set** (e.g. 6–8 videos + carousels + statics, varied styles) [MA2][MA1]. Refresh cadence: monthly (small budgets) → weekly (high budgets) [MA2].
- **Google ad copy** (RSAs): put the buyer's job in the **first 5 words**, lead with strong verbs, kill ego/fluff ("introducing", "all-in-one", "next-gen") [GA14]. Swap adjectives for hard metrics; use "You get [outcome] when [mechanism]"; results-based CTAs ("Get your quote" not "Submit"); anxiety-killing microcopy ("60 seconds, no spam"). Mine sales-call language for raw copy [GA14]. Feed RSAs **maximum asset variety** ("buckets of paint") — Google assembles per query; low per-asset impressions are fine [GA15]. **Term exclusions + matching restrictions** fence off-limits words in auto-generated copy [GA1].

## §measurement

- Governing law: **"good data in, good data out"** — Google/Meta algos are literal and only as good as their data [GA5]. Track only shallow raw form-fills and the machine finds **more cheap junk leads** [GA5]. In-platform numbers over-attribute (each platform credits itself) — don't take them at face value [GA5].
- Build a **measurement plan** mapping the real funnel (form → phone-screen → qualified → closed) with distinct conversion categories, not one generic label [GA5]. First-party data is the AI fuel — source claim **~30% performance lift** for first-party users; centralize via **Data Manager** (CRM/GA4/Shopify/sheets → Ads) with **confidential matching** (TEE) [GA6].
- **Google Enhanced Conversions** (web): hashes user-provided data to recover cookie-lost conversions (source claim ~+14%). **Enhanced Conversions for Leads (ECL)** + **Offline Conversion Import (OCI)** feed offline milestones back, unlocking **Value-Based Bidding** — assign real/proxy values (lead $100 → qualified $500 → closed $1,000) so the bidder chases quality, not the cheapest fill [GA5][GA6][GA3].
- **Meta**: run **Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI)** side-by-side for redundancy against ad-blockers/cookie loss [MA8][MA9]. **iOS14.5/ATT** gutted Pixel match rates and shrank Pixel/customer-list audiences [MA7]. Maximize **Event Match Quality** by sending more matched data points (name/email/IP/address) [MA9]. **AEM caps 8 prioritized events** per domain — prefer reusable **Standard events** over Custom Conversions to avoid burning slots [MA10]. **Attribution windows**: default 7-day-click; **add 1-day view** — Gen Z is ~2× likelier to buy without clicking, so click-only under-reports profit and starves optimization [MA11][MA12].

## §optimization

- **Search-term report > keyword report**: audit what users actually typed; loose/upper-funnel terms are priced lower by smart bidding to protect aggregate ROI — don't panic, be curious [GA1]. **Negative-keyword discipline** is the steering wheel for broad match (treat like shopping: add negatives constantly early, fewer over time) [GA2]. Negate wasteful dayparts (e.g. non-converting 12am–7am) via ad scheduling [GA11].
- **Message-match / congruence**: keyword → ad → landing page must share one theme or Google **demotes the ad in auction**; dynamic text swaps page headline to the query [GA8]. 3-second clarity check: an LLM (or human) should extract what/who/action in one pass [GA8]. Slow/mobile-broken pages bounce → lower quality → **higher CPC + worse position** (the "death spiral") [GA7][GA9]. Use priority keywords naturally for Quality Score, never stuff [GA10]. Don't dead-end the thank-you page — state exactly what happens next [GA10].
- Meta stat-sig: use a significance calculator before killing tests; make changes in windows (7–14 days), don't constantly tinker [MA-creative].

## §failures — where budget burns

- **Meta**: wrong objective (Traffic/Engagement → "clicky" non-buyers instead of Leads/Sales) [MA15]; **helicopter-parenting** edits that reset learning [MA16]; single/near-duplicate creative punished with high CPM [MA2]; Custom-Conversion misuse diluting signal + exhausting AEM [MA10]; ad↔page expectation mismatch [MA15]; and ignoring backend economics (a $35-LTV customer can't absorb rising CPCs) [MA19].
- **Google**: legacy SKAG sprawl starving smart bidding [GA1]; broken/shallow conversion tracking (the death spiral) [GA7]; Display budget leaking to cheap Audience-Network junk clicks [GA16]; feed-fragile Shopping (price/stock mismatch → disapprovals) [GA16]; PMax cannibalizing brand/organic to inflate ROAS without brand exclusions [GA16].

## §shared strategy

- **Offer beats tactics**: a strong offer wins with a mediocre campaign; no tactic saves a bad offer — zero traction is usually an offer-market-fit problem. De-risk (bundles, guarantees, honest scarcity, price transparency) over discounting; sell **time-saved/headache-avoided**, not feature stacks [MA1][MA-offer].
- **CAC:LTV is the real game**: the business that extracts most LTV (repeat/subscription/ascension/referral) can pay most to acquire and is unbeatable in the auction. Source claim: lifting retention 80%→90% doubles LTV, 90%→95% doubles again [MA20][MA19]. Front-end **ROAS compression is inevitable at scale — chase net profit, not vanity ROAS** (4× on $1M > 10× on $10k); many aim to **break even on front-end** and profit on the backend [MA19].
- **Paid vs organic**: paid = fast, direct-response, allows off-platform CTAs, but volatile/seasonal-cost; organic = slow to build, limited link reach, but serves indefinitely for free and builds trust cold ads can't buy — **run both in parallel** to protect margin [MA21].
