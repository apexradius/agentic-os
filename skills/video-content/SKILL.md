## What this skill is

A video-content partner distilled from a **Video Production** corpus (YouTube-creator retention/packaging
craft, 2024–2026). It turns "make video that performs" into an executable loop so a model working on video
pulls the right rule and *actions* it: locks the thumbnail+title *before* recording, scripts 80% story, wins
on audio over camera, cuts 70% to the best 30%, packages for the click and the session, and uses AI as an
accelerator on the routine middle — instead of algorithm-stuffing titles and publishing an unhooked, dead-air,
100%-AI-slop upload.

## The workflow

Run the stages relevant to the task — each cites a rule from the KB (§ + `[VP <id>]`).

### 1 — PACKAGE FIRST (§pre-production, §platform-delivery)
Decide the **thumbnail + title before recording** — they are the promise; if you can't write them the idea
isn't clear. Find the angle (skyscraper an outlier, niche-bend a proven format into a new market), not just a
topic. **Titles win on emotion/curiosity, not SEO** (<50 chars, outcome-not-process, plant a question);
**thumbnails ≤3 colors, never cover the hero, don't repeat the title**. **Never publish packaging blind** —
test in a mock mobile feed / A/B and let CTR decide.

### 2 — SCRIPT FOR RETENTION (§pre-production)
**80% why-it-matters (story) / ~20% how-to** — audiences don't sit through raw instructions. Lead every point
with a story before the fact, write in vulnerability/flaws (not a flawless hero), target **reading age 8-10**,
and draft in **triplet bullets read aloud**, not word-for-word (keeps delivery natural). Plant **open loops +
stakes** so the viewer stays to resolve a question.

### 3 — SHOOT: AUDIO OVER CAMERA (§production)
**Gear is not the bottleneck** (phones shoot great video); **audio quality beats video quality** — soft focus
goes unnoticed, bad audio is unwatchable (a cheap clip-on mic fixes it). One **key light ~45°** off-axis + a
bounced fill; a **low f-number** blurs the background in any room; use a **shot list** (wide context + detail
close-ups + 2-4 talking clips) and the **film-booth shuffle** for cheap setup variety.

### 4 — EDIT TO THE AVATAR (§editing)
**Cut >70%, keep the best 30%** (litmus: delete A→B and if it still makes sense, it goes). **Pace to your
avatar, not the trend** — manic ~15-cuts-in-20s hooks young viewers but alienates mature/complex-topic
audiences; use ~60s **pattern interrupts** either way. Kill dead air; **captions animated + high in frame**
(UI blocks the bottom); keep music from burying the voice. Retention is a **human problem** (resolve a
question / sustain an emotion), not a technical one.

### 5 — DELIVER, REPURPOSE, SUSTAIN (§platform-delivery, §distribution-repurposing, §ai-video)
Short-form dies at the **~1.5s Cliff** — open on a **Stop-Stack** pattern interrupt, no "hey guys". **Long-form
is the watch-time/revenue base** (claimed >70% of YT watch time; ~**30% short / 70% long** mix). Drive
**session time** to ONE next video. Run a **one-to-many** pipeline (one long-form → shorts/reels/newsletter),
build **native** audiences (external links starve the algorithm), and keep a **sustainable cadence** (one
weekly banger > seven rushed). Use AI on the **routine middle only** (10-80-10; it raises the floor not the
ceiling) — fact-check its output; **100%-AI slop gets demonetized**.

## Output contract
Return, in order:
1. **Package** — the title (<50char, emotional) + thumbnail concept, decided before production.
2. **Script** — the retention structure (80/20 story split, the hook / open loop, reading age).
3. **Production** — the audio/lighting/framing + shot-list call (audio-first).
4. **Edit** — the cut ratio + pacing-to-avatar + caption/pattern-interrupt plan.
5. **Deliver** — the platform (short Cliff / long base), session-time next-step, repurposing, and AI-assist boundary.

## Constraints (what NOT to do)
- **Never record before the thumbnail + title exist** — no clear package means an unclear, rambling video.
- **Never SEO-stuff a title or chase the algorithm** — the algorithm mirrors human emotion/curiosity; write for the human.
- **Never prioritize camera over audio** — bad audio is unwatchable; a phone + a cheap mic beats a nice camera with hiss.
- **Never copy trend pacing blindly** — pace to the avatar; manic cuts alienate mature/complex-topic audiences.
- **Never leave dead air, low captions, or a throwaway end screen** — cut >70%, captions high-in-frame, tee up one next video for session time.
- **Never open short-form with a greeting or waste the first ~1.5s** — Stop-Stack the Cliff or lose the swipe.
- **Never publish packaging blind or ship 100%-AI slop** — A/B the package; AI does the routine middle only (10-80-10), fact-checked.
- **Never fabricate color-grade/motion-graphics/cinema/live/licensing craft** — the corpus lacks it; defer generation to ai-video, assembly to video-produce.
