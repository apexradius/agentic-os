## §pre-production
- Concept beats topic: a great video is "not the idea but the way the idea is presented" — reframe standard topics into fun/clickable angles [VP 41ae2735].
- **Niche-bending:** niche = market × format; create a new niche by porting a proven viral format into a market that never used it (gaming tier-list → rank toothpaste). Only bend formats you can fill with real expertise [VP b3dddbb8].
- **Skyscraper method:** find a high-performing existing video, build "taller" via better quality, new info, or delivery [VP 1d282c7].
- Competitor analysis is "unreasonably effective"; model outlier videos, apply **80/20 imitation vs innovation**, put your own spin on it [VP 7b3d599f].
- Presentation formats that lift interest: challenge/speedrun, "mistakes that stop you," relatable story over dry how-to [VP 4fcf649c, VP 41ae2735].
- Decide thumbnail + title BEFORE recording — they're the promise/blueprint; if you can't write them, the idea isn't clear enough and you'll ramble [VP e2c9b3d].
- **Script for retention:** spend **80% on why it matters (story), ~10-20% on how-to** — audiences don't listen to raw instructions [VP 50377796].
- Lead every point with a story before the fact; raw stats are "instantly boring" [VP 50377796, VP 1d282c7]. Use visual metaphors to make info concrete [VP a5c2a37b].
- Write in vulnerability/flaws, not perfection — viewers connect with struggle, not flawless heroes [VP 4fcf649c].
- Target **reading age 8-10** (tabloid, not broadsheet) so nobody feels excluded [VP 4ad8b22b]. Draft in **triplet bullet points, not word-for-word**, and read lines aloud to keep natural delivery [VP 0dc39781, VP 64d75381].

## §production
- **Gear is not the bottleneck:** a phone shoots great video; major creators still film on phones. Don't wait on a "fancy camera" [VP fad0ff51, VP e2c9b3d].
- **Audio > video quality.** Out-of-focus footage goes unnoticed (creators' most-viewed videos are soft); but quiet audio boosted in post adds unfixable hiss and is unwatchable [VP 64d75381, VP 3ef6c177]. Even cheap wired EarPods have "remarkably good" mic quality; a clip-on wireless (DJI Mic) into phone/camera gives clean audio on the go [VP 8cd07aad].
- **Lighting:** one key light ~45° off the camera axis = bright face + soft shadow. White walls bounce a free fill; hang a dark panel for harsher contrast [VP 0603cb4, VP 463d72e].
- **Background:** never paint the set yellow (mucky skin reflection) — buy test pots first; frame out ugly elements (rental doors); rugs/soft furnishings tame echo [VP 0603cb4, VP 463d72e].
- **Low f-number lens** (f/1.8, below f/3.5) blurs the background even in a tiny room; more depth behind = more bokeh [VP 0603cb4].
- **Teleprompter as monitor:** drag a camera-preview or script onto an Elgato-style teleprompter to hold eye contact with the lens while seeing yourself [VP 463d72e]. Capture card turns camera into a webcam for framing/live [VP 0603cb4].
- **Shot list (vlog/business):** context (wide establishing) + detail (textures, close-ups, walking) + 2-4 talking clips (intro hook, mid reaction, wrap) [VP fad0ff51].
- **Film-booth shuffle:** film outdoors, present a few lines, spin camera to a new backdrop, repeat — 4 setups near-zero effort; in edit keep eyes in same screen position so cuts aren't jarring [VP a5c2a37b].

## §editing
- **Baseline cut:** trim clip heads/tails so there's zero dead air; auto-silence tools (Recut) export an XML into Final Cut/Premiere [VP a5c2a37b, VP 0fe94141].
- **70/30 rule:** best channels cut >70% of what they film, showing only the best 30% [VP fe1b246].
- **Cut litmus test:** if you delete a chunk between point A and B and it still makes sense, everything between should go [VP fe1b246].
- **Country-roads pacing:** change setup/angle every ~60s (turn head to 2nd camera, re-crop) or the viewer daydreams; not heavy b-roll, just pattern interrupts [VP 5d30ed5].
- **Pace to the avatar, not the trend:** ~14.75 cuts in the first 20s hooks young viewers but alienates older/complex-topic audiences who need slower digestion [VP a5b6da8b].
- **Screenshots/b-roll:** no slow drifting pans/zooms (can't read moving text); use sharp cuts and draw the eye to the key detail at the exact second [VP 82e008f1].
- **Captions:** reading is automatic, so on-screen words force engagement; animate/integrate them into the action (not lazy single-line scroll); place text **higher in frame** so TikTok/Shorts UI doesn't block it [VP 83e8ae2, VP 82e008f1].
- **SFX/music:** snaps/pops/beat-drops intensify pattern interrupts; keep music from burying the voiceover; obviously-fake SFX burn trust on raw/personal content [VP c4aaf6f4, VP 82e008f1, VP 83e8ae2].
- Retention is a **human problem, not a technical one** — people stay to resolve a question or keep feeling an emotion; open loops + stakes/forfeits plant multiple questions [VP fecd2139, VP 6b9ef572].

## §platform-delivery
- **Short-form (Shorts/Reels/TikTok):** viewers on autopilot decide to swipe in **~1.5s** — the "Cliff" (near-vertical retention drop). No "hey guys" greetings [VP fecd2139, VP c4aaf6f4].
- **Stop-Stack formula:** STOP the scroll with a visual/audio/text pattern interrupt, then STACK tension/curiosity immediately [VP c4aaf6f4]. First line should tell the whole story (hero, conflict, stakes); or foreshadow the outcome for an open loop [VP 4fcf649c, VP fecd2139].
- **Long-form is the watch-time/revenue base** — claimed **>70% of total YouTube watch time**; ideal channel mix **~30% short / 70% long** (shorts = "arms" reaching strangers, long = "legs" holding loyalty) [VP 4d63d65, VP 732cff0].
- **TV viewership rising:** lean-back audiences want polished, story-driven 30-60min narratives, not manic editing; "ultra-form" (2h+ essays/compilations) has a supply shortage and works as second-screen content [VP 49c63e7, VP e8d5197].
- **Titles:** don't SEO-stuff (kills homepage CTR — viewers click on emotion/instinct); keep **<50 chars** for mobile; frame outcome/proof ("I created a brand") over process, plant a question/paradox [VP e680eaa2, VP 9f3c82d4, VP bf636e6, VP 3db2ccbb].
- **Thumbnails:** ≤3 colors; never cover the "hero"; don't duplicate the title as text. 2026 styles cited: neo-minimalism (≥50% negative space), cinematic embedded text (3-4 words), whiteboard/framework, familiar-UI mimicry, candid-fakes, warped faces [VP f3c64cd, VP bf636e6, VP 88e1fd8c].
- **Never publish packaging blind:** test in a mockup feed on mobile; use title/thumbnail A/B testing and let real CTR data decide [VP e680eaa2, VP 2f62daa9].
- **Session time** is the prized metric — direct viewers to ONE relevant next video (not confusing multi-choice end screens); verbally tee up the next watch ~80% through [VP 732cff0, VP ce6fdb4f].

## §ai-video
- AI **raises the floor (average content) but not the ceiling** (exceptional). It's an accelerator, not a creator [VP 7070f64].
- **Good for:** ideation/hooks, SEO descriptions/tags, book/wiki summaries for research, trimming waffle, reading-age rewrites, silence-cutting, thumbnail concepts, slide gen (Gamma), prototypes (Lovable), auto-dubbing/translation, transcript-to-clips repurposing (Riverside co-creator) [VP 50377796, VP 4ad8b22b, VP 0fe94141, VP e680eaa2, VP d16a8ff0, VP 08f866e5, VP 463d72e].
- **Cannot do:** the beginning (your original vision) or the end (commercializing the output) — only the routine middle. Can't supply taste/craft/judgment (what to cut, what to keep, whether to publish at all) [VP 6d229ab2, VP 7070f64].
- **Hallucinates** — publishing AI drafts un-fact-checked burns credibility [VP de7dd9d7, VP 5f4fb4f6].
- **Faceless/AI-avatar channels** fight uphill: YouTube runs on human connection; exceptions (Kurzgesagt) need huge teams. Synthetic voices suit slow documentary/sleep content but raise credibility questions on conversational channels [VP adfd0813, VP 42a9626c, VP cd7252bc].
- **YouTube 2026 anti-slop crackdown:** platform stated "anti-lazy, anti-template, anti-mass-produced"; channels demonetized/removed for triple-threat repetition (carbon-copy thumbnails + recycled title templates + 2h AI-voice panning slideshows) [VP de7dd9d7, VP f296646b, VP 7070f64].
- **10/80/10 delegation rule:** you supply the first 10% (rich unique context), AI does the middle 80% (draft/organize), you finish the last 10% (taste, rewrite to your voice, fact-check, personal fingerprint) [VP 6428ac5, VP de7dd9d7].

## §distribution-repurposing
- **One-to-many pipeline:** one long-form → main video + multiple shorts + reels + LinkedIn + newsletter; searchable transcripts (Riverside) let editors slice on keywords [VP 463d72e, VP 42a1b2a].
- Enable the **"remixing"** upload setting so other creators clip you into Shorts = free discovery entry points [VP e680eaa2].
- **External-link trap:** don't paste YouTube links onto FB/IG — they register as "external views," starve the algorithm of CTR data, and viewers won't leave-app; build native audiences instead [VP f8530af].
- **Batch to kill context-switching:** dedicated filming days + a "hookbook" idea doc; pre-produce assets assembly-line style [VP 54613c4, VP 2da232f, VP 08f866e5].
- **Consistency = a sustainable schedule, not daily grind** — one weekly "banger" beats seven rushed uploads; daily posting speedruns burnout [VP bb31373c, VP e2c9b3d]. Cadence can run in cycles (burst on news/release, slow in quiet periods) [VP 7e863b7].
- **Volume compounds:** each upload is a lottery ticket; one viral hit triggers back-catalog binges → compounding channel asset [VP adfd0813, VP e25dbfd].
- **Content-wall reset** (stalled channel/new niche): pause 30+ days, then publish 3 long-form or 5 shorts at the same minute → shared recency cross-pollinates suggested traffic [VP 311f941].

## §failures
- **Designing for "the algorithm" over humans** — keyword/tag-stuffing titles; the algorithm just mirrors human click behavior (emotion/curiosity) [VP bb31373c, VP 3db2ccbb, VP ce6fdb4f].
- **Trying to appeal to everyone / random topic mix** → appeals to nobody; laser-focus a defined audience [VP e2c9b3d, VP e25dbfd, VP adfd0813].
- **Perfectionism freezing action** — polishing to 100% wastes time and stops you publishing; 80% ships and often wins [VP 64d75381, VP 9031482b].
- **Creating from "need" (scarcity/metrics panic)** — audiences feel the pressure and back away [VP b306175f].
- **Obsessing over early noisy analytics/trolls** — pivoting on a handful of comments wrecks what the silent majority loves [VP bb31373c, VP 64d75381].
- **Packaging errors:** not testing thumbnails in a real feed; repeating the title as thumbnail text; clogging/covering the hero; showing process not payoff ("bricks not the house"); inside-jokes only understood after watching [VP e680eaa2, VP ce6fdb4f, VP bf636e6].
- **Hook killers:** wasting the first ~1.5s on greetings/jingles/backstory; killing tension by giving the payoff away early; slow pans over readable text [VP fecd2139, VP 83e8ae2, VP 82e008f1].
- **"Blurry potato":** hitting publish at 100% upload while YouTube is still processing → early viewers get 360p [VP e680eaa2].
- **Editing:** leaving dead air; blindly copying manic fast-cut pacing onto mature/educational audiences; refusing to cut >70%; low-placed subtitles blocked by UI; throwaway end screens that leak session time [VP a5c2a37b, VP a5b6da8b, VP fe1b246, VP 82e008f1, VP 732cff0].
- **AI over-reliance:** 100%-AI scripts = detectable bland "slop," lost trust; mass-produced template farms → demonetization; forcing a shorts→long-form funnel (different viewer intent) alienates audiences [VP 9031482b, VP 7b3d599f, VP de7dd9d7, VP bb31373c].

## Gaps / honest caveats
- **YouTube monorail.** Corpus is almost entirely YouTube-algorithm/creator advice. TikTok/Reels covered only as re-cut destinations for the same footage; near-zero TikTok-native or LinkedIn-native craft. No treatment of ads-as-video, VSLs, or commissioned brand films.
- **No craft depth in:** color grading, motion graphics / After Effects / compositing, title design systems, sound mixing/mastering beyond "don't bury the voice." All "editing" here is retention-editing, not post-production craft.
- **No cinema / narrative / scripted film** — no coverage, blocking, continuity (beyond eye-line in the shuffle), directing actors, or multi-cam live switching. No **live-streaming / webinar** production.
- **No music/footage licensing or rights** — sources mention *avoiding* copyright (grab your own footage) but nothing on sync licenses, royalty-free libraries, or usage rights. No **releases/consent** for filming identifiable people, no legal/compliance layer.
- **No measurement/analytics depth** — CTR, retention curve shape, and "session time" named as levers, but no analytics workflow, cohort/audience-retention diagnosis, or attribution.
- **No budgeting/producing at scale** — no crew roles, call sheets, production scheduling, or client-delivery/versioning workflow. Gear advice is solo-creator desk-setup only.
- **Accessibility framed only as retention** — captions are treated as an attention hack, never as accessibility for deaf/hard-of-hearing viewers.
- **Vendor-tool specificity** — several claims are anchored to specific products (Recut, Riverside, Elgato, DJI, Gamma, Lovable, VidIQ). Treat as illustrative, not required.
