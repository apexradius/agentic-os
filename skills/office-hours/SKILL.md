# Office Hours — Idea Validation & Brainstorming

## Mode 1: Startup (Market Validation)

Six forcing questions (inspired by YC partner sessions):

1. **Demand Reality**: "Who is desperately looking for this RIGHT NOW? Not 'would be nice' — who is actively searching, willing to pay, frustrated by current options?"
2. **Status Quo**: "What do they do today without your solution? How painful is it really? What does it cost them (time, money, reputation)?"
3. **Desperate Specificity**: "Describe your ideal first customer in one sentence. Name, role, company size, specific problem, why they'd switch TODAY."
4. **Narrowest Wedge**: "What's the smallest possible version that solves the #1 pain? Not the vision — the first thing you'd ship in 2 weeks."
5. **Observation**: "What have you seen or experienced that others haven't? What unique insight makes you believe this will work?"
6. **Future-Fit**: "In 5 years, if this succeeds wildly, what does the world look like? Does that world feel inevitable?"

After answers: produce a 1-page assessment with go/no-go recommendation.

## Mode 2: Builder (Design Thinking)

For side projects, hackathons, open source:

1. **Problem**: What annoys you? What do you wish existed?
2. **Users**: Who else has this problem? (minimum viable audience)
3. **Solutions**: Brainstorm 5 approaches (quantity over quality)
4. **Filter**: Score each on feasibility (1-5) × impact (1-5)
5. **Prototype**: Define the 4-hour MVP scope
6. **Ship**: What's the fastest path to first user feedback?

Output: Design doc saved to `docs/[idea-name]-design.md`

## When to Use
- "I have an idea..."
- "Is this worth building?"
- "Help me think through this"
- Before writing any code on a new project
- Before `/spec-driven-dev` or `/planning`
