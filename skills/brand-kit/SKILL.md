# Brand Kit Generator

Creates a complete brand identity system for any niche/industry.

## Output Structure

### 1. Color Palette
- Primary (brand color) + contrast
- Background + foreground
- Surface (subtle bg for sections)
- Border
- Accent (secondary highlight)
- Sale/error
- Announcement bar (bg + text)

### 2. Typography
- Heading font (must be valid Shopify font handle — check against Shopify's font library)
- Body font (same requirement)
- Scale and weight recommendations
- Letter-spacing style (tight for luxury, wide for minimal, normal for friendly)

### 3. Shape Language
- Border radius values (0=luxury/sharp, 4-6=professional, 8-12=friendly, 20+=organic/soft)
- Button style
- Card style

### 4. Voice & Tone
- Brand personality (3-5 adjectives)
- Headline style (editorial, bold, friendly, technical)
- CTA language patterns
- Testimonial tone

### 5. Brand Story & Positioning
- **Transformation narrative**: Customer goes from [Zero State] → [Hero State] — brand is the guide, not the hero
- **Ownability Gap**: What visual/messaging space have competitors NOT taken? That's the brand opportunity.
- **PCP Influence Model**: Shift Perception → establish Context → achieve Persuasion
- **Hamburger Content Method** for brand content:
  - Top Bun: Relatable, non-salesy content
  - Patty: Direct product/service tie
  - Bottom Bun: Indirectly related value content

### 6. Imagery Direction
- Subject demographics and context
- Photography style (editorial, lifestyle, product, flat-lay)
- Lighting preference
- Color temperature
- Composition notes

### 6. Sample Copy
- 5 marquee/ticker phrases
- 3 hero headlines with subheadings
- 4 value propositions (icon + heading + text)
- 3 testimonials (with name + city)
- 4 FAQ questions + answers
- Newsletter heading + subtext

## Personal Brand Variant
When building a personal brand (creator, coach, thought leader):
- **Transformation over Information**: people pay for guided transformation, not knowledge dumps
- **Niche validation**: Can you serve this niche for 5+ years? Do you have unique insight? Is there a "Market of One" where you're the only obvious choice?
- **Content pillars**: 3-4 recurring themes that demonstrate expertise (not random topics)
- **Flagship offer**: price at the value of the transformation, not cost of delivery
- **Email sequence**: 4 emails → pitch flagship offer at end
- **Platform focus**: pick ONE platform, dominate it, then expand
