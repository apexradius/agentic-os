# Eval: brand-kit (rubric)

A brand identity is graded on completeness and internal consistency, not a single right answer.
Score against the rubric.

## Task
> "Create a brand kit for a premium cold-brew coffee startup targeting design-conscious
> professionals."

## Rubric
| # | Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Color system | 2 | Palette with named roles + hex; primary/text pairings meet contrast |
| 2 | Typography | 2 | A type pairing with a defined scale, not just font names |
| 3 ★ | Voice | 2 | Voice defined with concrete do/don't examples, not adjectives alone |
| 4 | Brand story | 2 | A transformation narrative (who they become), not a feature list |
| 5 | Imagery direction | 1 | Art direction / mood guidance for visuals |
| 6 | Consistency | 1 | All elements cohere to one positioning (premium + design-conscious) |

Total: 10 points.

## Pass threshold
≥ 8 of 10. A ★ criterion scoring 0 (no usable color system, or voice = bare adjectives) fails
regardless of total.
