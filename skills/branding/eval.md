# Eval: branding

A failing-baseline eval — without the skill the agent gives execution-first, taste-based branding advice
(pick nice colors, make a logo, post consistently, grow followers); with it, the agent works the strategic
tip of the spear, justifies visual choices on business logic + category codes, holds voice to close the
promise gap, and (for a personal brand) niches to a Market-of-One and monetizes transformation over vanity
reach.

## Baseline
Prompt the agent **without** the branding skill loaded:

> "I'm launching a consulting business under my own name. How should I build the brand — logo, colors, and how
> do I grow it?"

Observed baseline failure: the agent recommends "choose a clean modern logo, pick colors that look
professional (blue conveys trust), stay consistent, post regularly across all platforms, and grow your
following." Execution-first; color-by-taste with no business logic or category-code reasoning; no
positioning/differentiation; "post everywhere" spray; growth = follower count (vanity); no niche, no
monetization model, no key-person-risk awareness.

## Pass
With the branding skill loaded, the agent:
- Works the **tip of the spear** first — positioning, differentiation, audience — and treats the logo/colors
  as the replaceable **shaft**; frames premium pricing as being paid for judgment.
- Positions **different, not better** (blue-ocean), and justifies **color/category codes on business logic +
  demographic**, not "looks professional" — meet enough codes to be legible, break one to stand out.
- Defines **voice explicitly** (personality/tone/values) and frames it as closing the **promise-vs-perception
  gap**; authenticity ("scars not studies", messy middle) over polish.
- Niches to a **Market-of-One** (Ikigai intersection), rejects "post everywhere" for a **precision content
  funnel** (TOFU→MOFU→BOFU), **owns the audience** (email list via bridge asset), and **monetizes
  transformation** (value-based high-ticket, Curriculum+Coaching+Community).
- Replaces vanity metrics with **business metrics** (dollar-per-view, pipeline velocity, cash collected) and
  names **key-person risk** with the cog→orchestrator fix.
- Cites `[BR:/PB:<id>]`, frames numbers as directional, defers visual execution to `kole-jain-uiux` / copy to
  `copywriting`, and doesn't fabricate a Jungian-archetype or naming framework.

## Rubric (score each 0-2; pass ≥ 12/16)
1. Tip-vs-shaft: positioning/differentiation done before (not instead of) logo/color execution.
2. Different-not-better / blue-ocean positioning with intimate competitor awareness.
3. Color + category codes justified on business logic + demographic, not taste; legible-but-distinct.
4. Voice defined explicitly and tied to closing the promise-vs-perception gap; authenticity over polish.
5. Market-of-One niche (Ikigai) instead of broad/"post everywhere"; scars-not-studies.
6. Precision content funnel + own-the-audience (email/bridge asset) over follower-chasing.
7. Transformation-based monetization; vanity metrics replaced with business metrics.
8. Key-person risk named (cog→orchestrator); cites `[BR:/PB:<id>]`; gaps not fabricated; execution deferred.

**Fail** if the output is "nice logo, professional colors, stay consistent, post everywhere, grow your
following" — i.e. execution-first, taste-based, no positioning, vanity-growth, indistinguishable from the
no-skill baseline.
