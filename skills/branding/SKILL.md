## What this skill is

A brand-strategy partner distilled from two corpora — **Brand Strategy** (design-educator + entrepreneur) and
**Personal Branding** (creator-economy coaches), 2024–2026. It turns brand thinking into an executable loop so
a model doing brand work pulls the right rule and *actions* it: works upstream at the *tip of the spear*
(positioning/differentiation) where durable value lives, picks strategic color/category codes on business
logic not taste, holds voice consistent to close the promise-vs-perception gap, and — for a personal brand —
niches to a Market-of-One and monetizes transformation instead of chasing vanity reach.

## The workflow

Run the stages relevant to the task — each cites a rule from the KB (§ + `[BR:/PB:<id>]`).

### 1 — WORK THE TIP OF THE SPEAR (§brand-strategy)
Separate **brand** (strategic core: value proposition, values, audience) from **visual identity** (the outward
handshake) from **branding** (the active, consistent management). Durable value lives at the **tip** —
positioning, differentiation, customer insight, messaging; **execution is the shaft** (commoditized by
Fiverr/AI). Premium pricing comes from being paid for judgment, not production. **Brand equity is accumulated,
not bought** — repeat the system across every touchpoint or you get a "Frankenstein" brand. Feed the strategy
into any AI as a **brand memory system** so every output starts from one foundation (AI is a multiplier, not a
strategy — it just helps you move faster in the wrong direction).

### 2 — POSITION: DIFFERENT, NOT BETTER (§positioning-identity)
Escape red oceans (everyone claims the same fake-unique) for **blue-ocean** uncontested space. Respect
**category visual codes** (points of parity) — meet enough to be legible, break *one* to stand out; bucking
them too hard gets you rejected. Treat **color as a calculated strategic tool** justified by business logic and
demographic, never "it looks good." Know competitors intimately (reverse-engineer pricing/copy/history);
**re-segmentation can 10x** a brand. If archetypes come up, note the corpus has no Jungian model — position
your offer as the next chapter of the **story archetype your audience is already living**.

### 3 — HOLD VOICE, CLOSE THE PROMISE GAP (§voice-messaging)
Voice consistency is non-negotiable (define personality + tone + values explicitly — doubly so when an AI must
be told how to sound). Watch the **promise-vs-perception gap** — inconsistency and un-anchored automation widen
it. **Authenticity is the only bridge**: share the messy middle; audiences smell fake. **Greenwashing and
virtue-signaling backfire** — the promise-gap made visible dilutes trust.

### 4 — PERSONAL BRAND: MARKET-OF-ONE (§personal-brand)
For a founder/personal brand: **niche is a community, not a prison** — broad = broke, specific = sales; find
it at the **Ikigai** intersection (passion × skill × audience × monetization). Teach from **scars, not
studies** ("How-I", not "How-to") and **lean into quirks** — beige content is forgettable. Run a **content
funnel** (TOFU awareness → MOFU education → BOFU conversion), **own the audience** (route rented followers to
an email list via a bridge asset), and **monetize transformation, not information** (value-based high-ticket,
scale via Curriculum + Coaching + Community so results don't need the founder present).

### 5 — MEASURE REAL, MANAGE KEY-PERSON RISK (§measurement, §failures)
Reject **vanity metrics** ("monopoly money") for business metrics — dollar-per-view, *targeted* CTR/retention,
comment/DM depth, pipeline velocity (cash *collected*), testimonials. Name the **key-person risk** of a
personal brand (founder as growth-ceiling, identity fused to metrics) and the fix: **cog → orchestrator**,
package the methodology so revenue runs independent of the founder.

## Output contract
Return, in order:
1. **Strategic core** — brand vs identity vs branding, and the tip-of-the-spear positioning (not execution).
2. **Positioning** — the different-not-better / blue-ocean angle, category-code + color strategy (with business logic).
3. **Voice** — the explicit personality/tone/values and how it closes the promise-vs-perception gap.
4. **Personal brand** (if applicable) — the Market-of-One niche, content engine + funnel, own-the-audience + monetization.
5. **Measurement + risk** — the real business metrics to watch and the key-person-risk mitigation.

## Constraints (what NOT to do)
- **Never sell execution as strategy** — the logo/site is the shaft; durable value is the tip (positioning/differentiation).
- **Never justify a brand choice with "it looks good"** — tie every choice to the brand, the customer need, and the action it drives.
- **Never buck category visual codes so hard the brand reads as illegitimate** — meet enough to be legible, break one to stand out.
- **Never deploy AI/automation without a defined voice** — cold, un-anchored output widens the promise gap and damages the brand.
- **Never greenwash or virtue-signal** — the visible promise-gap dilutes trust and triggers backlash.
- **Never chase vanity reach or go broad** — broad = broke; precision niche + business metrics + own-the-audience.
- **Never fabricate a Jungian-archetype model, naming framework, equity-valuation formula, rebrand process, or brand-architecture doctrine** — the corpus lacks them; defer.
- **Never do the visual/UI execution here** — hand it to `kole-jain-uiux`; copy to `copywriting`; funnels to `cro`.
