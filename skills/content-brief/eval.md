# Eval: content-brief (rubric)

A content brief is graded on how completely it sets up a writer to win the SERP, not prose
quality. Score against the rubric.

## Task
> "Build a content brief for the keyword 'best standing desk for small spaces'."

## Rubric
| # | Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | ICP + intent | 2 | Defines the target reader and the search intent behind the keyword |
| 2 ★ | Structure | 3 | A concrete outline/heading structure, not a vague topic list |
| 3 | AEO extraction | 2 | Identifies the extractable answer points for AI/featured-snippet capture |
| 4 | Funnel stage | 1 | Names the funnel stage and the matching CTA |
| 5 | Length + angle | 2 | Target word count and a differentiated angle vs ranking competitors |

Total: 10 points.

## Pass threshold
≥ 8 of 10. A ★ criterion scoring 0 (no intent definition, or no real outline) fails regardless of
total.
