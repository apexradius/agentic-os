# Content Brief

Research and generate a comprehensive content brief.

## Steps
0. **Define ICP first** — Identify Ideal Customer Profile: use actual customer reviews, call notes, and forum posts to find the exact language they use (their words, not yours)
1. **Research intent** — WebSearch the keyword, analyze top 5 results for: format, word count, headings, questions answered
2. **Identify gaps** — What do competitors miss? What questions remain unanswered?
3. **Assign content type** (80/15/5 rule):
   - **80% Value/Educational**: teaches something, solves a problem
   - **15% Human/Behind-the-scenes**: builds trust and connection
   - **5% Promotional**: direct offer or CTA
4. **Assign ATLAS funnel stage**:
   - **A**udience/Awareness — broad, top-of-funnel
   - **T**argeted Problem — specific pain point
   - **L**ist/Lead Magnet — captures contact
   - **A**rouse/Nurture — builds trust over time
   - **S**ell — conversion-focused
5. **Copy length decision**: High existing trust + warm audience → short copy. Cold leads needing education → long copy.
6. **AEO Prioritization**: High value + low AI visibility = Immediate Priority; High traffic + high AI visibility = Maintain/Update; Low traffic + high AI visibility = Analyze for value
7. **Generate brief:**
   - Target word count (based on competitor average)
   - Suggested H1, H2, H3 structure
   - Key points to cover (with sources)
   - Internal linking opportunities
   - Schema markup needed (Article, FAQPage, etc.)
   - AEO extraction points (3–5 quotable sentences to target for AI citations)
   - Image suggestions
   - KPIs: organic traffic, AI citation rate, email opt-ins (if lead magnet), scroll depth, time-on-page
8. **Output** as structured markdown ready for a writer

## Anti-Patterns
- Ignoring mid-funnel nurture ("messy middle") — most content plans skip from awareness to sale
- Writing in brand voice instead of ICP language — use their exact words and phrases
- Publishing without AEO extraction points — miss the AI citation opportunity
