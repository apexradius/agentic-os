## What this skill is

A social-content partner distilled from **Social Media Marketing** + **Social Media Content Creation**
corpora. It turns organic strategy and short-form craft into an executable loop so a model making content
pulls the right rule and *actions* it: earns non-follower reach on "interest media," stops the scroll in
1.5 seconds, holds retention through the middle, repurposes one idea into many, and converts attention into
a DM/lead — instead of posting for the sake of posting.

## The workflow

Run the stages relevant to the task — each cites a rule from the KB (§ + `[SM/CC <id>]`).

### 1 — SET THE STRATEGY (§strategy)
Pick **one platform** where your buyers already are and master it (being everywhere = effective nowhere).
Treat feeds as **"interest media"** — distribution is by viewer interest, not follower count, so even a
tiny account gets non-follower reach. Know the **organic-vs-paid roles**: organic's superpower is *trust*
(raw/human beats polished, which now reads as AI and gets demoted) and it's *penalized for off-platform
links*; paid buys awareness and is the acceptable conversion tool. Define **3–5 content pillars** and an
**ICP** (mine call transcripts / switch-interviews) → speak to 3 core problems + 3 core desires on repeat.
Make the profile a **5-second-test** mini landing page.

### 2 — HOOK & HOLD (§content)
The hook is the first **1–3s**; the swipe decision hits at **~1.5s (the cliff)**. Use the **Stop-Stack**
(pattern-interrupt 0–1.5s → meaning+tension 1.5–3s) across visual/audio/text/conceptual hook types.
Script the open with **SPARK**, titles with **DRIVE**; **frontload the juiciest value**. Hold retention
with pattern interrupts (~every 2s), 3-shots-per-action, tightened silences, mood music + real SFX, and
**open loops** that pay off before planting the next question.

### 3 — REPURPOSE & PACKAGE (§content)
Treat each shoot as an ecosystem: slice one long-form into **10–20 shorts**, habit-stack native shorts
right after filming, batch on high-energy days. **Package** so title and thumbnail *complement* (two hooks,
never repeat); "doing beats teaching"; **A/B test titles + thumbnails** — don't guess.

### 4 — EARN DISTRIBUTION (§algorithm-distribution)
Algorithms reward **watch time + completion/low-skip** and **reach to non-followers**; a finished 60s beats
a half-watched 5-min. Work the **first-24h engagement window** (pinned question, reply to every comment).
Do **social SEO**: keywords on-screen + in description, front-loaded; respect platform hashtag limits
(IG ~5, not mega-lists). Ride only **on-brand** trends.

### 5 — CONVERT & MEASURE (§algorithm-distribution, §growth-measurement)
Run the **comment-to-DM funnel** (comment a keyword → auto-DM a low-friction lead magnet → conversation →
**own the audience via email**) rather than pushing off-platform links. Reject the **vanity-metric trap**:
track leads/source, cost-per-lead, ROAS, and *who* (titles/companies) engaged — one serious DM beats 10,000
passive views. Use **creators/micro-creators** for higher hook rates (one shoot, 3–5 hooks).

## Output contract
Return, in order:
1. **Strategy** — the one platform, the 3–5 pillars, the ICP's 3 problems/3 desires.
2. **The hook(s)** — Stop-Stack, with the hook type and the 1.5s tension named.
3. **Retention + package plan** — the mid-video tactics and the title/thumbnail pair (A/B variants).
4. **Distribution + funnel** — the social-SEO keywords, engagement-window action, and the content→DM→email path.
5. **Grade** — score against the checklist; name the single highest-leverage fix and the impact metric to watch.

## Constraints (what NOT to do)
- **Never spread thin across every platform** at the start — one platform, mastered, beats five half-done.
- **Never push traffic off-platform in organic** — organic demotes external links; convert via DM, then email.
- **Never optimize for vanity metrics** (followers/likes/raw views) — track leads, cost-per-lead, and who engaged.
- **Never over-polish to "look expert"** — perfection reads as AI and gets demoted; raw/human earns trust.
- **Never cliffhang without payoff, or blind-chase trends** — deliver the promise first; ride only on-brand trends.
- **Don't do paid bidding/budget, CRO, or long-form sales copy here** — those are `paid-ads`, `cro`, `copywriting`; cite `[SM/CC <id>]` and frame numbers as directional.
