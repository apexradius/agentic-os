## §strategy

- **One offer, one platform, master it.** Don't be everywhere — spreading across IG/TikTok/FB/LinkedIn/YouTube usually means effective on none; pick the one platform where your buyers already spend time and post high-value content consistently [SM b193a122, SM 3a5deedf]. Platform fit: LinkedIn = networking-first, starts B2B conversations faster [SM 0128d648]; YouTube = long-form education/search engine with a long tail (content surfaces months later, unlike 48h-decay feeds) [SM dc3e34e9]; Pinterest = search platform (not social), strong for Gen-Z visual/shopping discovery [SM 4ff9ac75]; TikTok/IG for younger/B2C [SM b193a122].
- **"Interest media," not social media.** Feeds now distribute by the viewer's current interests, not who they follow — the grid is <3% of IG consumption; this gives free, high-upside reach to non-followers even from a tiny account (19 followers → thousands of views) [SM 27bae54c, SM 5fbbdcf5, SM d6c2dd9e]. Organic social = free brand-building; "it's free" is the biggest under-exploited opportunity [SM a3df1edb, SM 46eba612].
- **Organic vs paid roles.** Organic's superpower is **trust** (raw, imperfect, human/behind-the-scenes content — perfection now reads as AI "tell" and is algorithmically demoted) [SM 19805530, SM 64d8c9cd, SM f8e42fd4]. Organic is **penalized for driving off-platform** (external links demoted) — it's designed to keep you in-app [SM 88ec6a81, SM 0a8a47c4]. Paid buys instant awareness + is the acceptable conversion tool (drives to landing pages) [SM 022b4426, SM 88ec6a81]. **Midfunnel play:** post creative organically FIRST; when a post out-performs your norm, move it to paid to scale — media should amplify proven creative, not hide weak creative [SM 5fbbdcf5, SM 0c2935ce, SM 58720635].
- **Content pillars (3–5 recurring themes), rotate intentionally** [SM f8e42fd4]. Models in corpus: (1) **Funnel** — Educational/problem-aware → How-it-works/process → Proof+CTA [SM f0f80633]; (2) **Personal brand** — How you build / Who you are / How you live [SM 9fe990a6]; (3) **Buckets for longevity** — "one for you" (algorithm/income), "one for me" (creative), "one for life" (legacy) — prevents burnout [SM 4a8d8dd6, SM 8c95458e, SM b6d69800]; (4) **LinkedIn "album tracks"** — intro 10% / testimonial-ballads 10% / win-celebration rock 30% / relatable floor-fillers 30% / experimental 20% [SM 65698682].
- **ICP first.** Know the ideal customer cold — mine past call transcripts with AI, or run "switch interviews" with 10–12 recent, overjoyed customers who still viscerally remember the pain; start qualitative/psychographic, layer quant later [SM 0128d648, SM 1628a936]. Pick 3 core problems + 3 core desires and speak to those six on repeat [SM d6c1dc5d].
- **Profile = mini landing page / 24-7 storefront.** Must pass the **5-second test**: a stranger instantly grasps what you do, who you help, next step [SM f6b96719, SM 0128d648]. Align bio + website headline + ad + content to answer one core question the same way; be about the customer's outcome, not company brags [SM f8e42fd4]. LinkedIn: pin an "intro track" to the Featured section [SM 65698682].

## §content

- **The hook = first 1–3 sec; the swipe decision lands at ~1.5s ("the cliff")** — a vertical retention drop; if no tension by 1.5s you lose them [CC 501274c6, CC 26a9569a]. **Stop-Stack formula:** Stop (0–1.5s pattern interrupt that breaks the scroll rhythm) → Stack (1.5–3s text/SFX/statement that injects meaning + tension) [CC ffaecc4c]. Hook types: **visual** (object enters/snaps, wide/low angle, zoom, slow reveal), **audio** (snap/pop/beat-drop, unexpected silence, real diegetic sound, trending audio), **text** (on-screen keyword popup — most scroll on mute), **conceptual** (foreshadow the climax/outcome, "I created…" proof-not-lesson, "everyone says X but that's a mistake," paradox) [CC ffaecc4c, CC 190b13c4, CC 5d381ed2, CC 0579cdd7].
- **Scripting frameworks.** **SPARK** for the opening 15–30s: Specific audience · Problem · Audacious promise · Results preview · Keep curiosity alive [CC d0d866e6]. **DRIVE** for titles: Desire · Relatability · Intrigue · Value-promise · Emotion [CC d0d866e6]. **Frontload the juiciest value** (viewers may not reach the end) and **avoid verbally numbering points** ("tip #1") so you can re-order/cut in the edit [CC 0579cdd7].
- **Retention through the middle/end** is a human problem (trigger emotion / answer a question), not just a technical one [CC 501274c6]. Tactics: **pattern interrupt ~every 2s** (zoom, jump cut, text pop, prop) [CC 501274c6]; **3 shots per action** — wide (context) / medium / close-up — for volume + variety [CC 190b13c4, CC cf6221ae]; **shorten word gaps/silences to ~0.1s** and use the **overlapping-audio** method for pace (tools: Descript, CapCut) [CC 7cec1159, CC 0f8f2855, CC 190b13c4]; **layer mood music + real SFX** to lift average view duration (e.g. Epidemic Sound, searchable by mood) [CC b13941a7, CC 3ff7762d]; **open loops** (tease outcome, resolve later) [CC 501274c6]; **infinite-loop hack** — an <15s short whose end blends into its start can hit ~122% AVD (viewers loop before realizing) [CC 4f2ebc44].
- **Formats that travel.** Reframe "how-to" → **"how-I"** (personal case study; lowers on-camera pressure) [CC 8e5de4c8]. **POV vlog** = communicate how you *feel*, not just narrate ("this channel is about ___ through the lens of ___") [CC d666a948]. **Series / long-term challenge** = built-in storytelling machine — "what happens to them next?" drives return visits the algorithm rewards; string finished series into an hour masterclass [CC 73d54385, CC fb0394f2]. **Listicle/list layouts** shown visually early boost retention [CC 8fe12a6a].
- **Repurpose one idea across platforms.** Treat each shoot as an ecosystem: slice one long-form/podcast into **10–20 shorts** (isolate section → 9:16 → captions → cross-post TikTok/Reels/Shorts), turn moments into GIFs for the community tab, and plan long-form so sections stand alone [CC d114f6b4, CC 0f8f2855, CC 3c498f24, CC 16cb00e3]. **Habit-stack** the reps: right after filming long-form, film 3 native shorts on 3 points made [CC 16cb00e3].
- **Production/batching.** Film in short memorized **chunks** (delete bad takes on the spot — no giant blooper reels, less storage) [CC 8e5de4c8, CC fd380f90]. Batch on high-energy days; swap outerwear over a basic top to change looks [CC 7cec1159]. Passively build a **B-roll library**; track the pipeline in a Notion calendar and storyboard/rate clips in frame.io for editors [CC 7cec1159, CC 11c7101b, CC 5116024c]. Choose a **sustainable cadence** and batch the first 3–4 videos to stay ahead [SM 9662903d].
- **Packaging craft.** Title + thumbnail must complement, **never repeat each other** — title states the benefit, thumbnail triggers curiosity/proof (two chances to hook) [CC d0d866e6, CC 0579cdd7, CC 5d381ed2]. "Doing beats teaching" (`I created a $10k brand` > `how to design…`); a paradox title (`banned player → greatest`) makes viewers click to resolve tension [CC 5d381ed2]. Thumbnails: mobile-first, <5 words, high contrast, show your face for personal brands; zoom out / mockup-preview to check [CC 0579cdd7, CC d0d866e6, CC 1b8494e6]. **A/B test titles + thumbnails** — don't guess [CC 1b8494e6, CC d0cf41bc]. Captions: keep short, use as keyword hub + one CTA [CC 7cec1159]. **Save trending audio to a folder while scrolling** so posting-day isn't a scramble; Epidemic Sound avoids muted-video/copyright/ads-eligibility issues from native audio [CC cf6221ae, CC b13941a7].

## §algorithm-distribution

- **What algorithms reward:** watch time + **finish/completion rate + low skip rate** (play duration = watch time + finish rate — a finished 60s beats a half-watched 5-min); reach to **non-followers** ("recommendable") is the growth signal [SM 0e5480e1, SM 42e12a13, SM f86624ba]. TikTok's interest-based algorithm is the hardest arena (≈98% of views from non-followers) and its skills transfer to IG/YouTube [SM 0e5480e1]. Meta now **punishes creative similarity** (fatigued/near-duplicate creative → higher CPM; new "creative similarity/fatigue" metrics) — reward is fresh, diverse creative [SM bf2dd194].
- **Consistency > frequency; volume breeds quality.** More posts = more chances + more audience data + faster on-camera reps; but a stable schedule you can sustain beats manic bursts [SM 56c862fd, CC 94130a34]. Set a **minimum/maximum** (e.g. ≥1 flagship video/week; more is bonus) and batch to protect it [SM 8c95458e, SM 8e31cb86, SM b1d8fa45]. Results compound slowly — reps for ~40+ days before signal [SM b1d8fa45].
- **Engagement window.** First **24h** is critical — pin a comment with a question/CTA before publishing to stimulate early conversation [SM 074c6c5e]. **Reply to every comment** — builds trust AND triggers a loop: while a viewer types/reads replies the video keeps looping, inflating watch time; in early days, ask them a question to pull them back for a 2nd/3rd view [SM 2655f1e8, SM 0e5480e1, CC 94130a34].
- **Search / SEO-for-social.** Search intent is rising (esp. TikTok) — put keywords **on-screen and in the description** (TikTok confirmed off-screen/hidden keywords do nothing); **front-load the core keyword** in titles before the ellipsis cutoff [SM 8e31cb86, SM 0e5480e1, SM 7f0a5b3e]. Hashtags: **live on TikTok**, but IG cut the limit to **~5** and penalizes spam/irrelevant tags — modern AI reads the actual visual/audio/text, so mega-hashtag lists are obsolete [SM 8e31cb86, SM f3a2bbc4]. **AEO/GEO:** YouTube Shorts feeds Google's Gemini; LinkedIn articles/newsletters "live forever" for LLM citation — publish clear, structured, factual (grade 9–11) content to get cited [SM 58720635, SM 5f0017bd, SM 0c2935ce].
- **Trends/sounds:** only ride trends that fit your brand voice — chasing every one turns the channel into "junk"/"wallpaper" [CC 1632b72e].
- **On-platform funnel: content → DM → lead (don't send off-platform).** Meta wants conversations in DMs (FB/IG/Messenger/WhatsApp) — people reply to messages more than they fill forms [SM 39165b67, SM f86624ba]. Use **comment-to-DM automation** (e.g. ManyChat): post names a frustration → "comment [keyword]" → auto-DM a low-friction lead magnet (checklist/template/audit, not a course) → conversation. This is a **"yes ladder"** of micro-commitments (comment → DM confirm → download) that captures first-party data in-app [SM 82ea3190, SM 45a801ca, CC d114f6b4]. Then **own the audience via email** (lead magnet → welcome sequence of 3–5, then 1–3×/week) — if the platform vanished tomorrow, email is your only line [SM b193a122, SM dc56b9a1].

## §growth-measurement

- **Vanity-metric trap.** Followers/likes/raw views/impressions don't pay bills; a problem-solution post can be the week's *worst* on engagement yet drive ~$10k pipeline [SM 0128d648, SM f86624ba]. Shift **output metrics → impact metrics**: one serious DM > 10,000 passive views [SM f8e42fd4]. Cheap "traffic" can be the most expensive (Meta Audience Network = accidental taps on kids' games → clicks, zero buyers) [SM 2b28ed85].
- **Metrics that map to business:** leads + source, cost-per-lead, cost-per-purchase, ROAS; and qualitative intent signals — **who** (job titles/companies) viewed your profile, DMs, completed forms [SM d6c1dc5d, SM 354675bd, SM 0128d648]. Only ~24% of small businesses track ROI — tracking leads→sales→revenue monthly alone beats most competitors [SM d6c1dc5d]. Pick the **campaign objective literally** — Meta optimizes for exactly what you choose (traffic finds clickers, leads finds form-fillers, sales finds buyers); boosting optimizes only post-engagement [SM f94e2977].
- **UGC vs creators — distinct.** Customer UGC builds trust; **creators/influencers** carry pre-established authority and drive materially higher **hook rates** (% past 3s) [SM d90dba0a]. Tactics: negotiate **one shoot, 3–5 different hooks** (vary opening line/setting) for cheap A/B variety; run as **partnership ads** (dual brand+creator identity); **micro-creators** (~40k followers, ~$500) with the right niche audience often out-perform big brand-first spend [SM d90dba0a].

## §failures

- **Cliffhanger without payoff** — feels like a cheat, drives the swipe. A loop must deliver the promised payoff first, *then* plant a new question [CC 501274c6].
- **Manic inconsistency** — 3 videos then a month silent = "panic attack followed by burnout"; trust is built by a schedule you can sustain, not bursts [CC 94130a34, CC 1632b72e].
- **Copying big creators** — they trade on established trust; mimicking makes you "YouTube wallpaper." Study their structure/psychology (hook, promise, emotion), then translate to your voice [CC 1632b72e].
- **Blind trend-chasing** — turns the channel chaotic; you can go viral for something you never want to repeat [CC 1632b72e].
- **Too polished / too salesy / creating from "need"** — perfection now reads as AI and is demoted [SM 19805530]; over-performing to "look like an expert" reads rigid, and content made "for the metrics not for them" makes viewers back away. Create from want, not scarcity; honesty is what makes content feel effortless [CC 8e5de4c8, CC 5d5d2c84, CC 0ff0db01].
- **Posting for the sake of posting** — volume without intent/value lowers baseline credibility; before publishing ask what it teaches, who it's for, what action it drives — "if the only reason is it's Tuesday, delete it" [SM f8e42fd4].
