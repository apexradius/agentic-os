# Eval: email-draft (rubric)

Email/sequence copy is graded on persuasion structure, not a single right wording. Score against
the rubric.

## Task
> "Write a 3-email welcome sequence for new trial users of a project-management SaaS."

## Rubric
| # | Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Subject lines | 2 | Each email has a subject built on a formula (curiosity/benefit/clarity), not generic |
| 2 ★ | One CTA each | 2 | Each email drives a single, clear next action |
| 3 | Sequence logic | 2 | The 3 emails progress with a reason/trigger (welcome → activate → convert) |
| 4 | Personalization | 1 | Merge tokens / segmentation hooks used appropriately |
| 5 | Scannability | 2 | Short, skimmable structure; no walls of text |
| 6 | Send guidance | 1 | Timing/cadence for the sequence specified |

Total: 10 points.

## Pass threshold
≥ 8 of 10. A ★ criterion scoring 0 (generic subjects, or multiple competing CTAs) fails
regardless of total.
