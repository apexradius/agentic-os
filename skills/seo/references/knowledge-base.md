# SEO Knowledge Base

> Source-cited rules for AI agents running SEO audits and strategy. Pull the relevant section, action the rules.
> Citations: `(src: <NB> <id>)` — `NB` = `SM` (SEO Masters notebook) or `LS` (Local SEO & Google Business notebook); `<id>` = source-id prefix. Every substantive claim traces to a notebook source. Numbers are source claims, not independently verified fact; treat thresholds as directional.

## Keyword Strategy & Search Intent
- Classify every target query by intent; content format MUST match the dominant intent of the current top 3 results or it cannot rank (src: SM fcfc3034, SM 89badbef). Four intents w/ claimed share of Google volume: informational ~50%, navigational 34.6%, commercial 14.8%, transactional 0.8% (src: SM bf2408de).
- Read the live SERP before writing: e-commerce category pages = transactional (don't rank a blog); image pack = build visual/image-led page; "People also ask" reveals follow-up intent (src: SM fcfc3034, SM 70217b88).
- Serve the primary answer in the first ~10 words, then answer the searcher's next ~5 questions (src: SM d8e7657f). For mixed intent, target the dominant one but you may build both a product page and an informational post (src: SM 89badbef).
- Pick keywords that move the business, not just volume — "traffic potential" > raw search volume; a high-volume top-funnel term with no conversion path is a trap (src: SM 7933cfea, SM 89badbef).
- Difficulty: a single KD number is misleading (it only reflects referring domains to the top 10) (src: SM 487aeea7). Use the DR+10 rule — only chase keywords where a site at/below your Domain Rating + 10 already ranks in the top 5; ladder up as authority grows (src: SM d8e7657f, SM 9c5053b4). Quick filter: "lowest DR ≤ 30 in top 5" isolates winnable terms (src: SM 487aeea7).
- Escape saturated seeds: everyone researches the same seeds, so their low-competition terms are now mid/high (src: SM 47ce1a43). Generate ~20 unconventional seeds via LLM, then run through a keyword tool (src: SM 47ce1a43, SM f9b9956f).
- AI Overviews are eating informational clicks (claimed ~35% lost even at #1) — prioritize action/tool queries AI can't satisfy: filter matching-terms for `calculator, generator, tool, checker, tracker, estimator`; these still send clicks and attract links (src: SM 7933cfea, SM d1d913db, SM 3d6600a3).

## On-Page & Content SEO
- Topical authority is claimed the strongest on-page factor — stronger than domain traffic; keyword density does NOT correlate with rank (higher-ranked pages average lower density) (src: SM ddf79590). Cover the entity graph (all related concepts/entities), not repeated keywords (src: SM 863e387c, SM ddf79590).
- Use pillar-and-cluster hubs: one master pillar + hyper-specific sub-pages, all reciprocally linked (src: SM ddf79590). Never run competing/duplicate pages on one topic — it causes cannibalization; consolidate into one definitive guide (src: SM fc3b8808).
- E-E-A-T is the defensible moat vs AI-generated content: add author bios w/ verifiable credentials, prove firsthand experience (case studies, own data, before/after, specific wins/failures), cite high-trust sources, keep author name/bio/advice identical across web + YouTube + LinkedIn (src: SM 5a0714ff, SM ddf79590, SM fcfc3034). Contradicting yourself across platforms is flagged as a trust risk (src: SM 5a0714ff).
- Structure for chunking: engines read HTML top-to-bottom (tree-walking) and process page in chunks, so lead each H2/H3 with the exact question and answer it in the first sentence; ~44% of AI-Overview citations come from blogs that answer directly with no fluff intro (src: SM 885aa7ab, SM 5a0714ff). Short paragraphs, bullets, bold, comparison tables; if a human can skim it, AI can cite it (src: SM ddf79590, SM d0dad340).
- Turn proprietary stats/data into custom charts and tables — earns links + AI extraction (src: SM 3d6600a3, SM d0dad340). Add multimodal assets (images/video/charts) for higher citation rate (src: SM ddf79590).
- Freshness: keep a visible "Last Updated" date, refresh declining posts (find via Search Console traffic drops), re-date meaningfully; AI-cited content is claimed 25.7% fresher than normal results (src: SM 885aa7ab, SM de1ca326, SM fc3b8808). Put the current year in title tags for freshness-dependent queries (src: SM fcfc3034).
- Internal links = the only link equity you fully control; they pass PageRank and are the crawl roadmap (src: SM d8e7657f). Reciprocal hub linking shares equity so a backlink to one page lifts the whole hub (src: SM d1d913db). Use descriptive contextual anchor text (never "click here"); keep money pages ≤2–3 clicks from the homepage (src: SM 89badbef). Never leave zero-body-text pages — Google's title rewriter will pull spammy external anchors to title them (src: SM 751123a8).

## Technical SEO
- Indexation blockers first: a `noindex` meta tag or a `robots.txt` block disqualifies a page entirely (src: SM fcfc3034). To speed indexing of new/updated URLs, use Search Console URL Inspection → Request Indexing (a request, not a guarantee) (src: SM aa1c536d, SM a4a3f1e4).
- AI-crawler access: check `/robots.txt` isn't blocking `GPTBot` / `OAI-Searchbot` — claimed 5.9% of sites block GPTBot, often auto-added by security tools (e.g., Cloudflare since ~July 2025) (src: SM 885aa7ab, SM ef6a490d). Blocking cost Amazon a claimed ChatGPT-referral drop 18%→<3% (src: SM 4ced14f6). Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools — ChatGPT pulls from Bing (src: SM 7b8ccf01).
- JavaScript: Google renders client-side JS, but AI crawlers generally cannot parse content behind heavy JS — deliver static structured HTML or be invisible in AI answers (src: SM 7d46783e).
- Architecture: organize like a library (categories → subcategories); minimize crawl depth so important pages are reachable in few clicks to save crawl budget (src: SM fc3b8808, SM 89badbef).
- Schema (JSON-LD): baseline stack = Organization + Product/Service + LocalBusiness (if applicable) (src: SM bbb2c872). Define the exact entity in machine language, not marketing jargon ("HVAC specialist" not "innovative solutions") — claimed HVAC traffic doubled in 90 days, ERP rankings +40% in 60 days from correct entity schema (src: SM bbb2c872). Add FAQ/HowTo/Review schema so machines classify content (src: SM ddf79590).
- Core Web Vitals: optimize load speed, mobile-first — every second of delay costs conversions; strip non-essential page weight (src: SM ae2c7f89). [Sources emphasize LCP/INP/CLS optimization but do NOT state specific ms/s thresholds — apply external Google thresholds separately.]
- Redirects/canonicals: 301 (permanent) passes link signals + drops the old URL; 302 (temporary) keeps old URL indexed and does NOT consolidate equity (src: SM 751123a8). A 302 + missing canonical on a contentless page lets the title rewriter pull spammy anchor text into the SERP title (src: SM 751123a8). Never bulk-redirect old/acquired pages to the homepage (no topical relevance = wasted equity) — 301 each backlinked URL to a topically matched page (src: SM 31d767c9, SM 937f4443).

## Off-Page / Link Building
- Backlinks remain a top organic signal; as few as ~10 quality contextual links can move a stuck page (src: SM 6c7103b2). For AI search specifically, branded mentions on authoritative third-party sites have the strongest correlation with AI-Overview visibility — above backlinks, referring domains, and DR (src: SM 7eeafc46, SM 885aa7ab).
- Great content does NOT earn links passively — it must be actively promoted to people who can link (src: SM 9c5053b4). Avoid cheap/PBN/Fiverr links — spam-policy violation, can wipe ~85% of traffic; audits show noindex-page links = dead (src: SM ac37b475, SM 566549a8). Legitimate paid placements (digital PR, niche/local directories, NAP citations) are fine; e.g. premium legal directories ~$300–400/mo where all competitors are listed (src: SM 6c7103b2).
- Linkable assets: frontload quotable stats/definitions at the top of the page ("linkable points"); turn data into 3 clean shareable charts; build free tools (calculators/generators) into a reciprocally-linked tool hub — inherently useful, attract links at scale (src: SM 3d6600a3, SM d1d913db, SM 5aec6113).
- Outreach plays: (1) Outdated-stat — find highly-linked old stats via competitor anchors, publish fresh research, email everyone citing the stale number (src: SM 89badbef, SM 487aeea7). (2) Competitor-agnostic — Link Intersect for sites linking ≥3 competitors but not you, then personalized pitch (src: SM fc3b8808, SM fb8556bb). (3) Unlinked brand mentions — `"brand" -site:brand.com`, ask for the link (src: SM 487aeea7). (4) Link-intersect directories your competitors are in (near-100% success) (src: SM 5aec6113).
- Guest-post vetting filters: DR 30–65, ≥5,000 monthly organic visits, ≥500-word pages (src: SM 487aeea7). Reclaim lost equity: "Best by links" report → filter HTTP 404 → 301 to relevant live pages (src: SM 487aeea7).
- Anchor text: keep a natural mix (naked URL, brand, target keyword, title variations); over-optimized/spammy anchors confuse crawlers (src: SM 751123a8).

## Local SEO / Google Business Profile
- Local rank = Proximity + Relevance + Prominence; proximity is dialed up on mobile (src: LS 09a26c3a, LS 79784b35, LS d0f2258b). Prominence is driven by reviews + mentions and by offline branding that triggers direct brand searches (src: LS 79784b35, LS 5725b94c).
- Primary category is claimed the #1 local factor — pick the most specific one ("Air Conditioning Repair Service" not "HVAC Contractor"); use up to 9 secondary categories; swap primary seasonally where demand shifts (src: LS 8a3b0dbb, LS fbc70528). Wait ≥24h between profile edits and 2–3 weeks before judging a category change (src: LS 8a3b0dbb).
- Completeness: complete profiles claimed 5x views / 7x clicks vs incomplete; keep hours/address/phone accurate and set Special Hours for holidays (src: LS 447e722d, LS 5cafa099). Add predefined Services (ranking weight) + custom services (≤120 chars) for long-tail (src: LS 8a3b0dbb). Use attributes to catch conversational long-tail (src: LS 4a29ed66).
- Photos: real originals only — Google filters duplicate stock and deprioritizes AI-generated images; 90% of consumers likelier to visit a profile with photos (src: LS a21f7b1e, LS d0f2258b, LS 447e722d).
- Reviews (heavily weighted): hit 10 reviews (claimed algorithmic milestone — 9→10 jumps rankings/leads, 10→11 does nothing) (src: LS fbc70528, LS 525967ac). <20 reviews blocks conversion (~50% won't trust) (src: LS 1efa67ad). 74% of consumers only weigh reviews from the last 3 months; sustain ≥1 review/week (src: LS 1efa67ad, LS a3c4e110). Never batch/"bunch" reviews — velocity spikes trip fraud filters (30-day block or wipe); ask every customer for a steady trickle (src: LS 1efa67ad).
- Ratings: below 4.0 is a conversion killer (fix to ≥4.0, ideally 4.5); 4.7–4.9 is the conversion sweet spot; a perfect 5.0 reads as untrustworthy (src: LS a3c4e110, LS d7fa4dd7, LS bc8fa32d).
- Review keywords: keywords in the CUSTOMER's review text influence ranking/relevance (Google surfaces verbatims); keywords stuffed in OWNER replies do nothing (and look like gaming) (src: LS ad42f93d, LS a3c4e110, LS d7fa4dd7). Guide reviewers with prompts ("what did we fix for you today?") to elicit category keywords + photos — never dictate or incentivize customers; owner/manager (not agency/AI-boilerplate) should reply, within a week (81% expect it) (src: LS 09a26c3a, LS 1efa67ad, LS d7fa4dd7).
- Review order hack: photo reviews stay pinned far longer (claimed 249 vs 158 days) — to bury a pinned 1-star, ask happy past customers to add a photo to old reviews (src: LS 96ebf1d9). Negative-review replies: apologize/empathize, don't defend, own the valid part, state the fix, offer restitution (src: LS 3ef653c9). Review gating (routing unhappy customers to private forms) is banned — Google can wipe your review history (src: LS 1efa67ad, LS d0f2258b).
- NAP: keep Name/Address/Phone identical everywhere (Google cross-checks Yelp/Apple Maps/Facebook/aggregators); purge duplicate listings (one per directory) — duplicates drag down the primary (src: LS 79784b35, LS dc95d953, LS 44ef12ab). Putting the exact address in the global site footer gives a local ranking lift (for searchers inside that city) (src: LS 96ebf1d9).
- Local content: "Diversity Update" may demote the organic page linked from GBP — if organic tanks, link GBP to a different sub-page (src: LS 6ee69b21). Replace generic AI text with real local case studies (jobs, local pricing data, timelines, photos) — claimed +57% conversions; extend title tags with neighborhood modifiers (claimed +15% visibility even >200 chars) (src: LS 9dcbc7e1, LS fbc70528, LS d0f2258b). Prefer "affordable" over "cheap" (same intent, keeps quality signal) (src: LS d0f2258b). Post to GBP ≥1/week and sync social handles into the dashboard (src: LS 447e722d, LS 89df5dee).
- Service-area businesses: showing a real physical address is now recommended over hiding it — AI/LLMs scrape Google's front end and can't see hidden addresses (invisible in AI search); hiding also limits available citation directories (src: LS 1a9da5c7, LS 28968b73). Build unique service-area pages (real local jobs, reviews from that town, landmarks, permitting/zoning facts, local cost data) — target a proximity hierarchy region→state→city→neighborhood; NEVER mass-publish city-swap templates (Google won't index them; scale risks a manual penalty) (src: LS fbc70528, LS 1a9da5c7, LS 8275ede0, LS d7fa4dd7).

## AEO / GEO / AI Search
- Each engine has a distinct citation bias (~86% of top-cited sources unique per assistant): AI Overviews lean YouTube/Reddit/Quora + facts/comparisons; ChatGPT leans mainstream news/Wikipedia (claimed 500k-query mix: Wikipedia 20%, blogs 19%, news 17%, vendor pages ~2%); Perplexity leans niche/regional blogs (claimed 33% blogs) — diversify placements accordingly (src: SM 7eeafc46, SM 885aa7ab, SM 7b8ccf01, SM 322fbe55).
- Complexity is the moat: AI Overviews trigger ~23–24% on 0–3-word queries, ~48% on 3–5 words, ~77% on 6+ words; by intent ~45.9% informational / 17.8% commercial / 6.1% transactional / 1.5% navigational (src: SM b2e3bd6b, SM bf2408de). AI traffic is lower-volume but far higher-intent — claimed longer sessions, higher engagement, converting up to ~23x better (src: SM 322fbe55, SM 7b8ccf01, SM 773a2b5f).
- Win citations with QAE structure (Question heading → direct answer first sentence → evidence/stat/original research), scannable formatting, and multimodal assets (src: SM 322fbe55, SM 5a0714ff, SM ddf79590).
- Entities: Google maps entities (a unique, well-defined thing) not just keywords — make your entity machine-readable via JSON-LD (Organization/Product/LocalBusiness) so AI knows exactly what you are (src: SM bbb2c872). [Sources cover robots.txt AI-bot access but do NOT mention `llms.txt` — don't assert it from these sources.]
- Brand mentions > backlinks for AI visibility; ChatGPT weights freshness + citation velocity over legacy domain authority — frequent high-authority mentions over a ~30-day window can outrank established brands; AI also weighs sentiment, so ensure you're framed positively (src: SM 7eeafc46, SM 885aa7ab, SM d0dad340, SM 863e387c). Only ~19% brand crossover between top SEO and top ChatGPT results — treat AI visibility as a distinct workstream (src: SM 7d46783e).
